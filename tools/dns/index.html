DNS records

Every record a domain publishes, in one lookup — addresses, mail servers, nameservers, text records and certificate authority policy.

Resolved over DNS-over-HTTPS. No account, no key, nothing stored.

Notes

    What this cannot tell you.
    • This is one resolver's answer, cached. Another resolver, or the domain's own nameservers, may say something different — and a recent change may not have propagated.
    • Absent records are not proof of absence: a resolver can fail or truncate, and this reports what came back.
