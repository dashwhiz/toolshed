Colour contrast

Whether text of one colour can be read on another, by the WCAG measure — and when it cannot, the closest shade to what you chose that would pass.

Two colours, foreground first. Hex, rgb() or a CSS colour name.

    What this cannot tell you.
    • Whether the text is actually readable. The WCAG ratio is a formula over two colours; it knows nothing about the typeface, the weight, the screen, or the person reading. A thin light face at 4.5:1 can still be hard work.
    • Anything about the newer APCA measure, which models perception more closely and disagrees with this one — particularly on dark backgrounds, where this formula is known to be generous.
    • The suggested shade is found by mixing your colour towards black and towards white in one per cent steps and taking the first that clears the bar. That is a straight line through RGB rather than a true lightness path, so a slightly closer colour may exist. It is not the nicest colour either, and it may not suit the rest of your palette.
