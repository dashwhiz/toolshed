Hidden characters

Text can carry characters you cannot see — invisible spaces, letters borrowed from another alphabet, marks that reverse how a line reads. Paste anything and see what is actually in it.

Nothing is uploaded. The scan runs in this page.

        What this cannot tell you.
        
      
          
    • It reports what characters are present, not whether they are malicious. Zero-width joiners are normal in emoji, and plenty of scripts legitimately need marks flagged here.
      • 
          
    • The look-alike list covers common Cyrillic and Greek letters that imitate Latin ones. It is not the full Unicode confusables table, so an unusual substitution can slip past.
      • 
          
    • Clean text is not safe text. This says nothing about what the words are asking you to do.
